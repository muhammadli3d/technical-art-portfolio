## Goal

Cut iteration time from "request → terrain" to under a minute, while keeping
artist control over silhouette, biome distribution, and erosion direction.

## Approach

- Houdini Digital Asset wraps the full graph behind seven exposed parameters.
- Hydraulic + thermal erosion, with a paintable mask for art direction.
- Biome layers built from slope, altitude, and a low-frequency control texture.
- Python exporter writes heightmap, weight maps, and a scatter manifest in one click.

## Result

A scene that previously took half a day now lands in Unreal in roughly five
minutes, with clean handoff for environment artists to paint over.
