## Goal

Build a stylized lighting model that reads well on any silhouette, runs cheaply
on mobile, and gives art direction a single set of dials per asset.

## Approach

- Half-Lambert wrap term, biased toward warm tones in lit areas.
- Fresnel + view-aligned rim, gated by a curvature mask so it doesn't fire on flat panels.
- Single 256² matcap supplies highlight character — swapped per material family.
- Hand-painted detail noise modulated by world-space scale for cohesion.

## Result

Three test props share one master material with seven parameters. The fragment
cost stays under the project budget on a mid-tier mobile target.
