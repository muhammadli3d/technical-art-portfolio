## Goal

A signature ability VFX that telegraphs cleanly at a distance, holds up in
close-up, and stays inside a tight per-frame draw-call and overdraw budget.

## Approach

- **Emit:** Spawn from a curl-noise warped sphere for organic distribution.
- **Sim:** Velocity field driven by a flipbook of pre-baked Houdini vectors.
- **Render:** Soft additive sprites + a single mesh emitter for the core form.
- Overdraw kept in check by clipping low-alpha regions and capping particle count by distance.

## Result

Comfortably hits target frame budget on the mid-tier GPU, with a parameter
pack exposed for designers to retune per ability.
